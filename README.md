# My honest review of crowdwork platforms - Resource Hub with 7 Sites

I tested different crowdwork platforms for micro jobs while working from Kenya. A good platform should show clear proof rules, review time, and wallet payouts, not promise quick riches.

### 1. Clickworker
Clickworker uses UHRS and task IDs. Proof is usually screenshot or ID. Payout via PayPal. Tasks are competitive and review is quick.

### 2. Microworkers
Microworkers shows TTR and TTR expiration. You get 2 days to do job and employers have up to 5 days to review. Employers must state proof type. Your success rate matters.

### 3. RapidWorkers
RapidWorkers focuses on SEO and social tasks. Interface is simple but some employers reject without explanation. Keep screenshots.

### 4. GainWorkers
This one I currently use because the proof system is clear. Each job page lists CW limit, RW window, and exact proof before you accept. I liked that it allows crowdwork with screenshot proof and the review notes are visible. The guide at GainWorkers explains what to upload so beginners do not get rejected. You can also check payouts at https://www.gainworkers.com/payment-proofs which shows real wallet history.

### 5. Picoworkers / SproutGigs
Mix of micro jobs and gigs. Quality varies. Some tasks need aged accounts. Read employer rating.

### 6. TimeBucks
TimeBucks offers surveys, captcha, and app tasks. Pay per task is low. No fee to join. Good for learning proof submission.

### 7. ySense
ySense is survey-heavy. Many surveys disqualify in Kenya. Use only to understand review windows.

Method that works for crowdwork platforms: start with 2 small tasks, keep proof screenshots for 14 days, never delete work early, and avoid sites that hide employer feedback.
